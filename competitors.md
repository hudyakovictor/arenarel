## Key Market Insights

### Market Sizes & TAM
- **Casual crypto sims**: 50M+ downloads (Cryptomania); TAM confirmed: tens of millions; people REQUEST education
- **Binance Academy**: 36M visits/month = 26.8% of all exchanges
- **TradingView**: 239.4M visits/3 months
- **Crypto traders losing**: 70-95% in minus (community consensus on Reddit r/CryptoCurrency); Polymarket: 70% of 1.7M in minus; Top 0.04% take 70% profit ($3.7B)

### Competitive Gaps (Our Advantages)
1. **No one teaches DECISIONS before click** — All simulators teach EXECUTION; our `forward-after-answer` = their paid feature (ChartingLens $14.99/month)
2. **Evidence-based journal** — +12-18% risk-adjusted from systematic analysis; Trading Game's AI coach hallucinates
3. **Ethics = differentiator** — FCA/SSRN data: gamification of trade frequency harms retail; we gamify error analysis, not trade frequency
4. **KPI corridor is realistic** — Market D1 27-32% vs our 40; session 8-12 min and 2-4 runs = our design exactly (benchmarks 35-36)
5. **Streak "day with closed error"** — Only honest version of Duolingo mechanic: reward for process analysis, not for click fact
6. **Cosmetic-led monetization validated three times** — 42% IAP multiplayer; season $5-15/4-8 weeks; mid-spender = target, not kit
7. **P2E model studied** — v1 without payments and v3 proof-of-skill = only surviving sequence
8. **Phaser 4 risk mitigated** — Standard objects compatible, our stack (Graphics/Text/tweens/scenes) unaffected by breaking changes; Canvas deprecated — we already use WebGL; Upgrade to ^4.2.1 safe

### Underserved/Nich Markets (Our Blue Ocean)
- **No trading educator teaches DECISIONS before click** — All teach execution only
- **No trading educator has determinism + honest journal as core** — Trading Game's AI coach hallucinates, progress resets
- **No trading educator combines chess.com-style streak mechanics with trading education** — Validated quota model
- **No trading educator for RU-first audience with 10+ locale architecture from day one** — Binance 44 languages = main organic driver
- **No trading educator with cosmetic-led monetization that never affects data/answers/budget** — Fixed in contracts
